How Swimcloud Power Index Works

1. Base Times: Each event (SCY, SCM, LCM) is evaluated against NCAA Division I Championship standards.

2. Formula: Points for an individual event are calculated using:

Points = (((Time / BaseTime) ^ 3) - 1) * 100 + 1

3. Weighted Average: The overall Power Index uses your top 4 scoring events:

  • Events 1 & 2: 100% weight
  • Event 3: 25% weight
  • Event 4: 2% weight

Lower overall index scores reflect faster times relative to NCAA D1 standards.
